\section{Small Limits in \Set}

We construct small limits in $\Set$ explicitly as subsets of dependent products $\Pi(j \in \Ob_J).\ D(j)$ from Section 1.4.

\begin{definition}
A small limit in $\Set$ is a subset of a dependent product $\Pi(j \in \Ob_J).\ D(j)$ that satisfies the universal property of a limit.
\end{definition}

\begin{proof}
We prove that limits in $[C^\op, \Set]$ are computed pointwise, and that the Yoneda embedding $h_- : C \to [C^\op, \Set]$ both preserves and reflects all limits.
\end{proof}
