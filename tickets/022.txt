Proved the Uniqueness of Representation Theorem: if $(A, u)$ and $(B, v)$ are both universal elements of $G \in [C^\op, \Set]$ (or of $F \in [C, \Set]$), there exists a unique isomorphism $f \in \mathrm{Iso}_C(A, B)$ such that $\GMor^{B,A}(f)(v) = u$ and $\Phi_{B,G}(v) \circ_{[C^\op,\Set]} \eta^f = \Phi_{A,G}(u)$.
