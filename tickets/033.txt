Ticket 033: Right Adjoints Preserve Limits (RAPL) and Left Adjoints Preserve Colimits (LAPC) via Yoneda
Type: Mathematical
Status: RESOLVED
Backup before fix: backups/yoneda_agy_033.tex

Issues Identified & Resolved:
1. Proved via a chain of Yoneda natural bijections that if $L \dashv R$, then $R$ preserves all limits that exist in $D$ (RAPL) and $L$ preserves all colimits that exist in $C$ (LAPC).
2. Proved that the limit and colimit functors (when they exist for all diagrams $J \to C$) are right and left adjoints to the diagonal functor $\Delta : C \to [J, C]$ ($\mathrm{colim}_J \dashv \Delta \dashv \lim_J$).
