Ticket 026: Slice Categories C/A, Canonical Isomorphism \int_C h_A \cong C/A, and Functoriality of \int_C
Type: Mathematical
Status: RESOLVED
Backup before fix: backups/yoneda_agy_026.tex

Issues Identified & Resolved:
1. Defined the slice category $C / A$ over an object $A \in \Ob_C$ and proved the canonical isomorphism of categories $\int_C h_A = C / A$, identifying $\langle A, \id_A \rangle$ as the terminal object of $C / A$.
2. Proved that the category-of-elements construction extends to a functor $\alpha \in \Nat(G, G') \mapsto \int_C \alpha : \int_C G \to \int_C G'$ over $C$, which is an isomorphism of categories iff $\alpha$ is a natural isomorphism.
