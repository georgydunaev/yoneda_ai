Proved the Universal Element Characterization Theorem: $(A, u)$ is a universal element of $G \in [C^\op, \Set]$ (meaning $\Phi_{A,G}(u) : h_A \xrightarrow{\sim} G$ is a natural isomorphism) iff for every $X \in \Ob_C$ and $v \in G(X)$, there is a unique morphism $f \in \Hom_C(X,A)$ such that $\GMor^{A,X}(f)(u) = v$.
