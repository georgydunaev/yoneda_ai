Issues Identified & Resolved:
1. Defined Kuratowski ordered pairs $\langle x, y \rangle \eqdef \{\{x\}, \{x, y\}\}$ and proved the characteristic ordered-pair theorem $\langle x_1, y_1 \rangle = \langle x_2, y_2 \rangle \LRA x_1 = x_2 \land y_1 = y_2$.
2. Explicitly defined $\dom(g)$, $\textrm{ran}(g)$, and relational composition $f \circ g$, and proved that functional relations are closed under composition with $(f \circ g)(x) = f(g(x))$.
